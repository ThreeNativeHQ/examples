SCOUT TWO / FLIGHT MANUAL

Know your aircraft.

Force-based flight: bank the wings to turn; the nose and flight path are not the same. Avoid hard pulls at low speed. Stability assist (U) helps with bank and trim; Direct mode allows free rolls and manual pitch trim. The battle pauses while this manual or the intelligence map is open.

Dive bombing: climb above 5,000 ft, approach the contact, nose down and deploy F. Put the amber impact circle on the moving deck, press B, and pull up before the water fills the screen.

Recovery: approach a friendly carrier from behind, aligned with its bow. Gear down, below 600 ft, under 140 knots, and within 700 m astern: press L to fly an assisted final, not teleport. Lower flaps with N; aim for 95–105 kt and a gentle descent. Any steering cancels the assist. A safe touchdown catches a wire and decelerates on deck. Service takes 12 simulation seconds.

Torpedo run: the torpedo preset uses a TBD-inspired airframe. Fly level, gear and flaps up, 16–50 ft above the sea and 68–110 kt. The green release cue confirms a safe envelope, not a guaranteed hit. Lead moving ships; your straight-running weapon needs 180 m to arm. Poor releases may break on water entry.

Battle damage: oil and fuel leaks drain real fuel; wing fires weaken spars and spoil lift. A damaged engine loses power. I closes the engine fuel valve and may help an engine fire, but stops the engine and does not extinguish wing-tank fires. Break off and return early.

Available while stopped on deck.
