# Platformer sweep brief

Build a small, playable 3D platformer from the supplied `reference.png`.

- Use a third-person camera behind and above the player.
- Make the player run, jump, collect a visible line of coins, and reach a goal.
- Include at least one raised platform, one gap, one enemy or hazard, and a restart path.
- Match the reference's bright sky, saturated green platforms, warm wood, and rounded toy-like silhouettes.
- Show the core objective and current collectible count in a readable HUD.
- The sealed proof uses `Space` for jumping and `ArrowRight` for the run.
- The sealed proof reads resource `state` paths `state.jumps`, `state.peakRise`, `state.playerX`,
  `state.coins`, `state.goalReached`, and `state.respawns`.

The first playable screen must be visible without a user account or external asset service.
