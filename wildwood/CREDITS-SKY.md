## Why this one

The game is a temperate coniferous valley in soft morning light. Forest Slope is Poly Haven's
coniferous-forest HDRI shot in soft morning summer light: low contrast, dappled sun through tall
pines — the same niche the scene's procedural sky and `setupForestLighting` were painted to fake.
